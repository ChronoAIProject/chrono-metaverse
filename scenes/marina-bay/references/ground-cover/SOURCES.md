# 滨海湾草地扫描素材

修剪草坪采用 Grass Ground，滨海中心和滨海步道的种植带采用 Leafy Grass，浮尔顿路分车带采用 Sparse Grass。扫描来自 Poly Haven，按 CC0 分发。草类选择和现场覆盖程度为视觉估计。站点的草坪底色、种植范围和大尺度明暗沿用已有的采样。每组的颜色、法线和粗糙度使用相同取样坐标。

- [Grass Ground](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground)
- [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass)
- [Sparse Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass)

## Compacted aggregate

The north bank uses a three-metre scan of gravel and exposed soil. The Marina Centre yard uses a two-metre scan with finer gravel. Both use the CC0 colour, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. The 768 px colours and 512 px surface maps share one orientation.

- Gravel Ground 01 has a transfer curve in the distributed normal PNG. Decode the components using its 0.45455 gamma, resize in linear space and normalize the vectors. The resulting median tangent direction is approximately (−0.01, −0.02, 0.98). [Gravel Ground 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_ground_01)
- Gravel Floor 02 has neutral tangent components in its original normal image. [Gravel Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor_02)
