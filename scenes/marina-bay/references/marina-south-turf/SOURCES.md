# Marina South turf reflectance

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2026). The four Sentinel-2 L2A acquisitions are 13 June, 17 August, 22 August and 1 October, tile 48NUG. The colour image uses 10 m surface reflectance, normalized to healthy turf separately on each date. Red, green and blue store a linear gain with 128 as unity. Most of the image has unit gain. The georeference in the scene records the three outer pixel edges of the UTM rectangle. [Copernicus terms](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions) · [Earth Search collection](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-l2a)

The parcel extract derives from OpenMapTiles landcover polygons supplied by OpenFreeMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The reviewed parcel mask contains the grass, grassland and meadow footprints visible along the scene routes. The script insets footprints by 10 m before estimating colour, requires valid observations from multiple dates and extends measurements only within their parcel. [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/)

Run `python scenes/marina-bay/scripts/bake-turf-survey.py CACHE OUTPUT.png` with NumPy, Pillow, rasterio, pyproj, SciPy and Shapely. The crop covers easting 372240–375980 and northing 140200–142740 in EPSG:32648. The selected 60% log-ratio strength was evaluated on 31 October and 22 November, both excluded from the bake. On 31 October the city view's median-centred RGB mean absolute error dropped from 0.343 to 0.261 stops across 3,030 visible cells. The corresponding Helix Bridge result was 0.368 to 0.322 across 2,313 cells. In the 22 November Sands view the error rose from 0.229 to 0.236 across 170 cells.

## Absolute daylight colour

The RGB channels retain the original parcel-to-parcel variation. Alpha encodes survey coverage as 128 outside the 48 accepted parcels and 255 inside, with a one-cell transition. At full coverage the material multiplies the decoded RGB gain by (0.867054, 0.785275, 2.672289). These values apply to the current grass scan and daylight.

The fit compares linear material albedo rendered from three registered rooftop cameras with the June, August and October training acquisitions. Ground intersections are grouped by 10 m cell. Vegetated comparisons use clear SCL class 4 and NDVI above 0.45. The multiplier retains 78% of the median log gain across cameras and dates. For the independent 31 October image the median absolute blue error falls from 2.04–2.07 to 0.65–0.67 stops across 1,000–2,243 visible cells per camera. November provided insufficient clear cells in these views. The red/green ratio error increases in all three photographic regions, by 0.017–0.128 stops. The regions contain 1, 15 and 74 comparable photo samples respectively. The blue-channel rank correlation across satellite cells also falls from 0.35–0.43 to 0.22–0.35. The linear RGB reflectance of a satellite pixel also depends on the lighting and viewing geometry.

## Gardens by the Bay vegetation coverage

The park boundary is OpenStreetMap relation 10231144, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The exposure mask uses the same Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A crop as the turf survey. It adds the 31 October and 22 November acquisitions to the four earlier dates. At each 10 m cell, take the median NDVI over at least three valid land observations (SCL 4 or 5). Ramp vegetation weight from 0 at 0.42 to 1 at 0.66. Reproject to a north-up 4 m Web Mercator grid with bilinear interpolation and fade over the inner 8 m of the park boundary. The source observations have 10 m resolution. The PNG covers 36.47 equivalent hectares after weighting. [Park relation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/10231144) · [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [Copernicus terms](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions)

Run `python scenes/marina-bay/scripts/bake-turf-survey.py --park-vegetation CACHE OUTPUT.png`. The generator prints the normalized Web Mercator rectangle used by the terrain shader. Two registered rooftop photographs guided the exposure. Their sky-relative mean absolute errors on eroded surface masks went from 1.830 to 1.487 stops (6,711 pixels) and 2.080 to 1.810 (1,899 pixels). The lakeside photograph contributed only 37 comparable interior pixels. The selected maximum adjustment is −0.75 stops, weighted by the multi-date coverage and the original imagery's green chromaticity.
