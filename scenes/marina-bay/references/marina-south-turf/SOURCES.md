# Marina South turf reflectance

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2026). The four Sentinel-2 L2A acquisitions are 13 June, 17 August, 22 August and 1 October, tile 48NUG. The colour image uses 10 m surface reflectance, normalized to healthy turf separately on each date. Red, green and blue store a linear gain with 128 as unity. Most of the image has unit gain. The georeference in the scene records the three outer pixel edges of the UTM rectangle. [Copernicus terms](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions) · [Earth Search collection](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-l2a)

The parcel extract derives from OpenMapTiles landcover polygons supplied by OpenFreeMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The reviewed parcel mask contains the grass, grassland and meadow footprints visible along the scene routes. The script insets footprints by 10 m before estimating colour, requires valid observations from multiple dates and extends measurements only within their parcel. [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/)

Run `python scenes/marina-bay/scripts/bake-turf-survey.py CACHE OUTPUT.png` with NumPy, Pillow, rasterio, pyproj, SciPy and Shapely. The crop covers easting 372240–375980 and northing 140200–142740 in EPSG:32648. The selected 60% log-ratio strength was evaluated on 31 October and 22 November, both excluded from the bake. On 31 October the city view's median-centred RGB mean absolute error dropped from 0.343 to 0.261 stops across 3,030 visible cells. The corresponding Helix Bridge result was 0.368 to 0.322 across 2,313 cells. In the 22 November Sands view the error rose from 0.229 to 0.236 across 170 cells.
