# Raffles City exterior finishes

The four rectified strips show balcony stacks, office windows and cladding from Diego Delso’s photograph of 18 August 2023. The strips and adapted model are available under CC BY-SA 4.0. [Original photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swiss%C3%B4tel_The_Stamford,_Singapur,_2023-08-18,_DD_18.jpg) · [Licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Building outlines and heights are from the OpenFreeMap building layer, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The model reuses the visible facade sections on the other elevations.
