# Outdoor timber decks

The pier, floating landing, dining terraces and pool decks use two CC0 scans. Each sheet includes sixteen courses of boards with different lengths and staggered ends. Diffuse colour, tangent normals and roughness share each board's source coordinates. The 1024 × 512 colour sheets cover four original UV periods along the boards and two across them. Their mean linear-light colour matches the preceding site material. Board cuts, weathering and gloss approximate the daytime appearance.
