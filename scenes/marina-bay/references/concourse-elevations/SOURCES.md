# Concourse facade photography

The balcony and office window sheets use a daylight photograph of the Concourse and Concourse Skyline taken in Singapore on 20 February 2015. The source is “Singapore The-Concourse-Office-Tower-and-Concourse-Condo-01” by CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. [Photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/?curid=39424571) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

The lossless source crops and adapted facade textures are distributed under the same license. The crop rectangles in `sheets.json` are measured in pixels of the 5525 × 3633 original. The bake selects contiguous runs of storeys within each vertical stack and estimates material colour, roughness and relief from the glazed openings and cladding. The material properties and projections onto the mapped footprints are visual estimates.
