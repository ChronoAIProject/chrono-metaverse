field.webp 用于地球全息材质。红通道是 Natural Earth 1:50m 陆地多边形的海岸有符号距离，绿、蓝通道分别来自地表亮度和夜间灯光。球面的方向和半径根据场景已有地图计算。

- [Natural Earth 陆地边界，公有领域](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-physical-vectors/50m-land/)
- [地表亮度来源](https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg)
- [夜间灯光来源](https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_lights_2048.png)
